# SCF Handbook Digest (Paysmith Memory)

**Source:** https://stellar.gitbook.io/scf-handbook (fetched live 2026-08-22)  
**Rule:** Re-read this + primary handbook before any SCF/Instaward submission. Do not invent deadlines.

## Entry path (all new projects)

1. Submit **Interest Form** on [communityfund.stellar.org](https://communityfund.stellar.org)
2. Panel reviews: ecosystem value, eligibility, team readiness
3. If eligible → invited to **SCF Build** round (email with deadline)
4. Parallel: engage local **Ambassador Chapter** for **Instawards**

## SCF Build Award (up to $150k XLM)

### Three tracks

| Track | Who | Decision |
|-------|-----|----------|
| **Open** | Novel use cases, strong teams | Panel + **Community Vote** |
| **Integration** | Compose existing Stellar building blocks; need existing traction | Category Delegate Panel |
| **RFP** | Match open RFPs for tooling | Panel |

### Tranches (4 payments)

| Tranche | % | Typical gate |
|---------|---|--------------|
| #0 | 10% | Award acceptance |
| #1 | 20% | MVP |
| #2 | 30% | Testnet (+ threat model/monitoring plan as of Aug 2026) |
| #3 | 40% | Mainnet / for Integration: **panel-ratified onchain metric** (NAV or volume) |

- Each subsequent tranche must be submitted within **90 days** of last payment or remaining funds forfeited (notify SCF if delayed).
- Lifetime Build cap typically **$150k XLM**; case-by-case up to $300k for previously funded.

### Referrals

Not mandatory but **heavily weighted**. Get referrer code from SDF employee, partner, or community member.

### KYC/KYB

Required before award disbursement: [stellar.org/bd-kyc](https://stellar.org/bd-kyc). May be re-requested anytime.

## Instawards (via Ambassador Chapter)

**Source:** Instawards Official Rules (handbook)

- Not open application — through **local Chapter Lead**
- Active engagement required (meetups, demos, collaboration)
- **30-day sprint** scopes
- Typical first award: **$1,000–$5,000** XLM
- Aggregate cap: **up to $15,000** XLM with follow-ons
- Review: usually 3–5 business days after Chapter Lead submission
- Path: Instaward → Build Award / accelerators
